STORYVERSE LIVE CAMERA AR UPDATE

WHAT CHANGED
- Opening a story immediately requests camera permission and starts the rear camera on supported mobile browsers.
- The live camera feed appears behind the Three.js story scene.
- NEXT SCENE rebuilds the scene-specific 3D environment while keeping the camera stream running.
- Each of the three stories has three different 3D environments (nine total).
- The original story QR URL format (?story=story-slug) is unchanged. Existing QR codes can keep opening the same stories.

INSTALL
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload/replace all website files in your existing GitHub repository.
3. Keep firebase-config.js and firestore.rules exactly as included; your existing Firebase values/rules are preserved from the supplied original ZIP.
4. Commit and push. Wait for Cloudflare Pages to deploy.
5. On each phone, open the deployed site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). If it shows an old version, close the tab and reopen the QR link; the service worker cache version has been bumped.

TEST
- Scan an existing story QR.
- Allow camera access when asked. The live camera should appear behind the 3D scene.
- Tap NEXT SCENE. The camera should remain on while the 3D environment changes.
- Repeat for all three stories on iPhone and Android.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This implementation is an in-page live-camera AR overlay. It overlays each 3D story diorama on the live camera image and keeps the camera open across scene changes. It does not yet use SLAM/image-target tracking to pin the model to a physical table or floor, so the scene is camera-relative rather than a persistent world-anchored object. Camera access requires HTTPS and user permission. Some mobile browsers may require a tap on START LIVE AR if automatic camera permission is blocked.
